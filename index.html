Questions

What firms ask before switching

Why not Clio, MyCase, Karbon or TaxDome?

Clio and MyCase are built for law firms, and Karbon and TaxDome for tax and accounting firms. Clio and MyCase include accounting for a law firm’s own books and trust accounts, not tax preparation or bookkeeping for clients. Bractel has modules for estate planning, probate, trust accounting, tax preparation and bookkeeping. A client you serve three ways is still one client.

Is switching painful?

Every firm goes through the same five steps: we match what you have today, we bring everything across, we give it your shape, you go live, and we check nothing was missed. You keep working the whole time. Nothing old is switched off until you’ve seen the new system hold everything. We move you in, and we check our work.
